Outcomes & learning

I’ve discovered a massive variation in the sensory output of objects across a wide range of area. The most notable outcome of my research was discovered while documenting trends in the taste and texture of objects across space. Objects found in the same location, within a variable area depending on location type, tend to have similarities with each other. Ex: Items that I’ve found in the ‘kitchen’ all seem to taste pleasant, while objects found in the grass tend to have adverse effects when consumed.

Research Experience for Undergraduates: Galactic Archaeology and the Formation of Elements

Not only was this my summer research project but it was part of my capstone (? make separate??) Spent a couple months reading and rereading beginning literature on galactic archaeology, taking notes and trying to understand the relevant stellar processes. Was given a prewritten galactic chemical evolution model code by one of Dr. Michael Hayden’s earlier postdocs and reviewed it to understand the notation and how the gce model was written and how it functioned. Part of this process was me learning how to work github, as well as have multiple local files for this and access them from remote locations. This took up most of the time thus far tbh it was very dense. I then began to isolate the Barium production in varying degrees based on metallicity and mass of agb stars to emperically find yields that better fit observational data. I spent time writing up not only these findings but a methods section of a paper for how the model worked. hope to finalize the paper by next year and submit for publication

Research Experience for Undergraduates: Adiabatic Shortcuts In Closed and Open Driven Two Level System

Over the course of the summer (2024) I had the privilege of working under Dr. Doerte Blume and her research group. I worked closely with Dr. Sagarika Basak (post-doc at the time) who’s work was a more complex variation of my own summer research project. I read through assigned literature relevant to driven two level systems, lewis-riesenfeld invariants, and adiabatic shortcuts. The first half of my summer was spent understanding these pieces of literature and more importantly replicating some of the key results in them by coding in Python to better understand how these systems work. The latter half of my summer I delved into more complex research papers and attempting to create my own end goal for an adiabatic system and solving for a sufficient shortcut. Throughout the summer I presented at weekly meetings in the research group, assembling short presentations going over my progress and it opened the floor to a collaborative work environment that encouraged a better understanding.
